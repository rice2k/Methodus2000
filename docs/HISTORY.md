# Methodus2000 / Methodus Toolz History

This repository preserves the history of **Methodus Toolz**, an AOL-era all-in-one utility suite distributed through **Methodus2000.com** and related mirrors.

## What Methodus was

Methodus combined AOL/AIM chat utilities, color faders, macros, IM tools, games, file/network utilities, customization, and other scene-era features in one interface. Surviving original pages also document tools such as Crapster, FTP client/server functions, a web server, CD ripping/burning, an IM manager, macro tools, screen-name utilities, and other AOL/AIM helpers.

Some historical functions would now be considered abusive, invasive, or malicious. They are documented here for preservation and research context only.

## Known creators / project identity

Historical pages and later archival references associate Methodus Toolz and Methodus2000.com with **ChiefDreegFleer** and **Jump0ffABuilding**, under the **Fungus Eye Entertainment** name. Surviving references also identify Methodus Toolz as a project dating from the 2000–2002 AOL-program era.

## AOL-era cultural context

Methodus was part of the broader late-1990s / early-2000s AOL "prog" scene. Contemporary and retrospective accounts describe all-in-one programs such as Methodus, ICYHot, Final Destination, and Toad as gateways into AOL chat-room customization, automation, scripting, and the underground software culture that grew around AOL.

## Research standard

This archive distinguishes between:

- **Original-source evidence**: surviving Methodus2000 pages, archived downloads, screenshots, and update pages.
- **Mirror/catalog evidence**: later AOL prog archives and mirrored ZIP catalogs.
- **Retrospective evidence**: later articles, posts, videos, and community recollections.
- **Unverified claims**: information that has not yet been corroborated by an original page or preserved file.

When possible, dates and claims in this repository should be tied to a preserved URL or file hash.
